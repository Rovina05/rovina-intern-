# Professionalism Reflection

## Research & Learn

### Examples of professional vs unprofessional behaviour in a casual work environment
Professional behaviour includes replying to messages in a reasonable time, being clear and 
respectful in communication, showing up prepared to meetings, and admitting mistakes honestly. 
Unprofessional behaviour includes ignoring messages for long periods, being dismissive or rude 
to teammates, gossiping, or blaming others instead of taking accountability.

### What does respectful communication look like in remote teams?
Using a friendly and clear tone in messages, avoiding sarcasm that could be misread over text, 
addressing disagreements calmly and directly instead of talking behind someone's back, and 
being open to feedback instead of reacting defensively.

### How can you give and receive feedback professionally?
Focus on the action or task rather than the person, assume good intent instead of assuming 
someone is being difficult, and ask clarifying questions if feedback feels unclear instead of 
reacting emotionally.

## Reflection

### Have you ever experienced or witnessed great teamwork and professionalism? What made it effective?
Yes, this happened at a previous job when I was waitressing for a huge event. It could have been 
really hard to manage with so much going on, but our manager was really prompt and understanding 
about everything. We were also all very vocal about what was needed, or if anyone needed a break. 
That combination of a supportive manager and open communication within the team made a 
potentially stressful situation run smoothly.

### What steps can you take to make sure your communication is clear, respectful, and inclusive?
I can keep my messages direct and simple, ask questions when something is unclear instead of 
guessing, and be mindful of tone in written messages since it can be easy to misread.

### How can you help create a positive, fun, and professional work environment?
By being respectful, staying open to feedback, being willing to help teammates, and keeping a 
positive attitude even when things get difficult.

### How can you prepare effectively for meetings with supervisors and colleagues?
By reviewing what I have completed beforehand, noting down any questions or blockers I want to 
raise, and being ready with updates so I don't get caught off guard.

### What would it look like to be proactive during your internship?
Asking questions early instead of waiting until I'm stuck, updating my supervisor on progress 
without being asked, and looking for the next task instead of waiting to be told what to do.

### How might you follow up with colleagues or supervisors if you need information/action from them?
I would send a polite follow up message after a reasonable amount of time, like a day or two, 
and if I still don't hear back, I would follow up again or ask during a stand up meeting. If it 
is urgent and I still don't get a response after multiple attempts, I would escalate by reaching 
out to my supervisor directly or through the appropriate contact.

### What are some things you won't do?
I won't ignore messages for long periods, be dismissive of feedback or 
avoid taking responsibility when I make a mistake.
