# OHS Laptop Ergonomics

## Research & Learn

### What are the risks of using a laptop without an external monitor or keyboard?
Laptops force a trade off between neck posture and wrist posture, since the screen and keyboard 
are attached together. If the screen is at the right height for your neck, the keyboard ends up 
too high for your wrists, and if the keyboard is at a comfortable height, the screen ends up too 
low, forcing you to look down and strain your neck. Over time this can lead to repetitive strain 
injuries, neck pain, and wrist discomfort.

### What ergonomic equipment can improve posture when working on a laptop?
A laptop stand or riser to raise the screen to eye level, an external keyboard and mouse so your 
wrists can stay in a neutral position, and an ergonomic chair that supports an upright or slightly 
reclined position.

### What adjustments should be made to monitor height, chair position, and desk setup for a healthier workspace?
The top of the screen should be roughly at eye level, so you are not tilting your neck down. The 
chair should support your lower back, with your feet flat on the ground and elbows at around a 90 
to 100 degree angle. The keyboard and mouse should be at a height where your wrists stay straight, 
not bent up or down.

### What are some daily habits that reduce the impact of prolonged laptop use?
Taking short movement breaks roughly every 30 minutes, checking your posture throughout the day, 
looking away from the screen periodically to reduce eye strain, and avoiding working with the 
laptop flat on your lap for long periods.

## Reflection

### What equipment changes can you make to improve your workspace setup?
Since I currently only use my laptop at a desk and chair without an external monitor or keyboard, 
I could get a laptop stand to raise the screen to eye level, along with an external keyboard and 
mouse so my wrists stay in a more natural position instead of typing directly on the laptop.

### What behavioural changes can you implement to improve posture and reduce strain?
I can sit upright with my back supported by the chair, keep my feet flat on the ground, and take 
regular breaks to stand up and move instead of sitting in the same position for hours.

### How can you remind yourself to maintain good posture and take breaks throughout the day?
I can use Focus Bear's built in movement break reminders during my work sessions to prompt me to 
stand up, stretch, and check my posture throughout the day.
